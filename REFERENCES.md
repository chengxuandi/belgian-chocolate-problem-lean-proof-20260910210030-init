# Related work and correction

- V. Blondel, *Simultaneous Stabilization of Linear Systems*, Lecture Notes in Control and Information Sciences 191, Springer, 1994. DOI: [10.1007/3-540-19862-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-19862-8).
- V. Blondel, M. Gevers, R. Mortini and R. Rupp, “Simultaneous stabilization of three or more systems: conditions on the real axis do not suffice,” *SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization* 32 (1994), 572–590. DOI: [10.1137/S0363012991218815](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0363012991218815).
- Li Wang, Long Wang and Wensheng Yu, “Some Open Problems on Simultaneous Stabilization of Linear Systems.” This survey is cited as related background in the project literature review; it does not supply a complete determination of the Belgian Chocolate threshold.
- Z. Charles and N. Boston, “Exploiting algebraic structure in global optimization and the Belgian chocolate problem,” *Journal of Global Optimization* 72 (2018), 241–254. DOI: [10.1007/s10898-018-0659-5](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10898-018-0659-5); [arXiv:1708.08114](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.08114). The paper reports the comparison lower bound 0.9808348.

Existing theory establishes threshold structure. The preserved Lean project does not determine the threshold value. The separate certified lower-bound repository contains a finite exact witness for its own admissible parameter and makes no complete-solution claim.
